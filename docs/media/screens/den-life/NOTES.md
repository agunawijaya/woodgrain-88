Critique rounds after the hero frames, at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 390×844:

1. Posters ran under the top bar at 1280 (moved down and shrunk); a stray dashed ring showed at
   the jacket's top left (hidden until a patch is held); the Versus setup form was one tall
   column (now two, the raffle pool on the right).
2. The Run intro looked empty in the first second (the TV warming up; fine once settled); the
   calendar's close button sat under the page; on a phone the jacket overlapped its drawer and
   the raffle shelf pushed the chalkboard off the screen.
3. Accessibility and speed: printed words inside SVG buttons moved beside them; patch tiles named
   by what they show; the weekday labels darkened to AA at night; patches drawn as pictures so the
   jacket stays fast on a software renderer.
