## The cartridge

"32 in 1" (catalogue number CX26163P), released by Atari in 1988 for Europe and other markets
outside the United States. Facts used on the About page:

- Released by Atari in 1988; not sold in the US but in Europe and other countries; bundled with
  some consoles in the UK; boxed copies with a manual are rare; a different game loaded each time
  the system was switched on. Source: Centre for Computing History,
  <https://www.computinghistory.org.uk/det/37482/32%20In%201/>.
- The PAL image is 64 KB, holding 32 programs of 2 KB each: verified from the owner's copy.
- Two of the 32 are near-duplicates of others (59 and 45 bytes differ), so there are 30 distinct
  games; the second road-crossing copy changes lane lines, colours and the hero; the second fishing
  copy changes colours and anglers and swaps which difficulty switch handicaps which angler:
  verified from the files (lead-checked research notes, not published).
- The cartridge has no menu or selection code: each game is a complete program and the game is
  chosen by the cartridge at power-on; RESET only restarts the current game: verified from the
  files.
