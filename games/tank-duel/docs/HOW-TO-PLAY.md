# Tank Duel — How to play

## Goal

Hit the other vehicle more times than it hits you before the fuse burns down, or be first to 10
hits. On every channel, one hit is one point.

## Controls

Playing alone (against a Kid, in practice or in the tutorial), the whole keyboard is yours: the
arrow keys or WASD steer, Space or Enter fires. Two players on one keyboard split it: player 1
keeps the arrows, player 2 takes WASD.

| Action                                                     | Player 1 | Player 2 | Gamepad                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------- | -------------------------------- |
| Turn left / right                                          | ← / →    | A / D    | Left stick or d-pad left / right |
| Drive forward (tanks); afterburner or faster (aircraft)    | ↑        | W        | Stick or d-pad up                |
| Slow down (aircraft); back away (tanks, with Reverse gear) | ↓        | S        | Stick or d-pad down              |
| Fire                                                       | Enter    | Space    | A / Cross                        |
| Pause                                                      | Esc      | Esc      | Start                            |

Tanks turn in sixteen steps, as in 1977, and only drive forward. With the _Reverse gear_ setting
on (not in 1977), pulling back backs a tank away at half speed, for the Kids too. Aircraft never
stop. With the
_Cartridge wiring_ setting on, biplanes fly as the 32-in-1 cartridge wired them: up and down turn,
left and right set the speed.

## Rules

- **One shell each.** Each side has one shell in the air at a time, and your own shell never hits
  you, unless the _Own ricochets_ setting is on (not in 1977). Then a shell that has bounced off a
  wall at least once can come back and hit the tank that fired it, and your rival gets the point.
  A shell straight from the barrel, one you curl round with a guided turn, or one carried round a
  wrapping sky still never hurts you. It only matters on the bouncing and billiard channels.
- **Kinds of shot.**
  - Guided shells turn whenever you turn, so you can steer them round cover.
  - Straight shells fly where they were fired.
  - Machine guns fire short repeating bursts while fire is held.
- **A hit.** The struck vehicle spins for about two and a half seconds and is shoved back along the
  shot. Nobody steers while it spins, both shells vanish, and the clock keeps running.
- **Walls.** Tanks are pushed back off walls and each other, and slide free when wedged.
  - On bouncing channels a shell bounces off walls and comes back out of corners. Each bounce
    gives it back most of its flight, but no shell flies longer than one and a half lifetimes in
    all.
  - On billiard channels only a shell that bounced first scores.
- **Invisible tanks** show only while they fire, bump into something, or spin after a hit.
- **In the air** everything wraps at the edges, and nothing collides: not clouds, not other
  planes. A plane in a cloud is hidden.
- **Formations** (2 v 2, the bomber against three, one jet against three) move as one. Shots come
  from the leader, and a hit on any member scores.

## Modes and channels

From the game menu:

- **Vs a Kid**: you against the Neighbourhood Kid you pick from their photos.
- **Versus**: two players on one keyboard, two gamepads, or one of each.
- **Practice**: the other tank sits still, as an unplayed tank did in 1977. Nothing is recorded,
  and the aim line (a setting) can show where a shell would bank.
- **How to play**: a playable tutorial in six short steps.

Daily runs, challenge links and the Power Cycle Run's micro-challenges start straight from the
Den. Choose the channel with the TV dial above the game menu; the scoring rule (Timed round · 2:44

## Settings

The game's own settings, from the game menu:

- **Scoring:** timed round or first to 10.
- **Cartridge wiring:** off by default.
- **Aim line in practice:** off by default.
- **Explosions:** full or reduced.
- **Team colours:** orange and blue, or a colour-blind-safe pair.
- **Engine sound and effects:** two volumes.

Camera shake, appearance (Light, Dark or System) and the controls live in the Hall's settings.
Reduced motion turns off the shake and shortens the smoke.

## Scoring

- One point per hit; Ricochet Royale gives a point per bounce, and Last Tank counts rounds won.
- **Timed round:** the higher score when the fuse burns out wins, and equal scores are a draw. The
  fuse sparks faster and the plates blink in the last 20 seconds.
- **First to 10:** the first to ten hits wins.
- Your record on a channel is the most hits you have scored there in one match. Higher is better.

## Den Life

- **Handicap (the difficulty switches)**
  - A handicap evens out a match between players of different skill, like giving odds in chess.
    Take one when you play someone younger, give one to a Kid to make it easier, or take one
    yourself for a challenge. The game menu shows each side's handicap, with a (?) that explains it.
  - Switch A gives that seat a handicap: shorter shots (about five eighths) and slower aircraft,
    biplanes one speed and jets two. Tanks drive the same.
  - Switch B is no handicap. Both start on B.
  - Set them on the console in the Den, or from the pause menu. A flip applies from the next
    match.
- **Gold thread:** every patch is sewn in gold when you earn it with your switch on A. Grandpa's
  Respect needs A anyway.
- **The Neighbourhood Kids** see only what you see, use the same handicaps and steer with a
  person's stick:
  - Dee rushes in close and fires early.
  - Moss waits in cover, dodges shells coming her way and takes only clean shots.
  - Pip wanders, lets fly at whatever is in front and curls guided shells about wildly, with the
    occasional two-wall bank.
  - Grandpa Lou turns slowly but judges range, lead and banks off two walls almost perfectly.
  - Rosa baits you with curling shots and feints.
  - Benny learns your favourite lanes and turning habits over the match and cuts them off.
- **Power Cycle Run:**
  - Hit 3 times (Guided · Open Field);
  - Land a bank shot (Bouncing · Open Field);
  - Survive invisible: get through without being hit (Ghost guided · Quarter);
  - Down a biplane (Guided · Clouds);
  - Jet duel: 2 hits (Guided jets · Open sky).
- **Versus Night:** Tank Duel takes part. Seat 2 can be a person or a Kid.
- **Daily share line:** one square per hit in order, 🟩 yours and 🟥 your rival's (the last 12), then
  the score, e.g. `🟩🟩🟥🟩 · 3–1`.

## Tips

- **Guided shells:** fire past the cover, then turn. The shell holds its line until it is clear of
  the wall, so turning too early only flies it into the bricks.
- **Bank shots:** look for a wall at the same angle on both sides of the shot. A shell bouncing
  off a long wall comes back on the mirror of its approach.
- **Moving targets** are hard to lead; stop in cover and wait for them to cross your line.
- **Invisible channels:** every shot gives you away. Fire, then move.
- **In the air:** a cloud hides you. Use the brake to let a chaser overshoot.
