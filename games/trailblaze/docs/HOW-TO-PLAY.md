# Trailblaze — How to play

_The player's manual: goal, controls, rules, channels, settings, scoring, patches and Den Life._

## Goal

Steer a rider that never stops. Every cell it leaves becomes a wall. Make your rival run into one
before you do. The first rider to 10 points wins (5 on a short match).

## Controls

| Action                                          | Left rider (seat 2)          | Right rider (seat 1)                | Gamepad                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Steer                                           | W A S D                      | Arrow keys, or keypad 8 4 6 2       | Stick or d-pad           |
| Diagonals (eight-way channels)                  | Q E Z C, or two keys at once | Keypad 7 9 1 3, or two keys at once | Stick or d-pad diagonals |
| Leave a gap / rub out (gap and Doodle channels) | Space                        | Enter                               | A                        |
| Keep a Doodle as a picture                      | X                            | X                                   | —                        |
| Pause                                           | Esc                          | Esc                                 | Start                    |

Playing alone, you ride the right rider on the arrow keys.

A turn takes effect on the next step. A tap between two steps is never lost: the last direction
you pushed is kept until the step. While a turn is waiting, a small arrow by your score shows it.

## Rules

- Both riders move one cell at every step and can never stop. The cell a rider leaves becomes its
  trail.
- Ride into a wall, the border, your own trail or your rival's, and your rival scores a point. If
  both crash on the same step, both score.
- You cannot turn straight back on yourself, unless your difficulty switch is on A: then the turn
  goes through, into your own trail.
- Riders pass through each other. Meeting head-on still ends badly a moment later: each rides into
  the trail the other has just laid.
- After a crash there is a short pause, then a new round on a clean board.
- On the winning point the board freezes as it is, and the match is over.

## Modes and channels

From the game menu, pick a channel, then a mode. The card at the top right shows the riders of
the look you have chosen, up close; the pre-match cards show who rides which before the
countdown.

- **VS a Kid**: you against the Neighbourhood Kid you pick.
- **Versus**: two people, one keyboard.
- **VS Computer 1977**: the cartridge's own rider, on the left.
- **Doodle**: draw with your trail, alone or together. Nothing crashes and nothing scores.

The fourteen cartridge variations are channels, numbered as on the cartridge:

On a climbing channel, the pace steps up every 5.12 seconds of a round, from 3 cells a second to
nearly 17. Open edges let you ride off one side and back in at the other.

**Remix channels** open as you earn patches:

- **Fading Trails**: trails melt away 8 s after they are laid.
- **Ghost Trails**: trails vanish from sight after 3 s but stay solid. Remember where they were.
- **Shrinking Arena**: the walls close in one cell every 6 s.
- **Boost Lanes**: on the glowing lanes you move twice as fast.
- **Four Way**: four riders, one from each corner. The last one riding scores.

## Settings

- **Look**: Neon Grid, Toy Trains, Mummy's Wrap or Ice Trail. The rules are the same in all four,
  and you can switch from the pause menu mid-match.
- **Match length**: first to 10 (the cartridge) or first to 5.
- **Camera**: Overhead shows the whole board. Chase follows your rider closely when you play alone.
- **Colour-blind riders**: amber and periwinkle instead of orange and blue. Riders and trails also
  differ in shape and pattern, whichever colours are on.
- **Reduced effects**: calmer crashes and no camera shake. Always on when your system asks for
  reduced motion.
- **Speed-up preview**: the five paces of a climbing channel, side by side.

## Scoring

One point per crash, to the rider who did not crash; one point each when both crash together. First
to 10 (or 5) wins, and 10–10 is a draw. The results name the winner and their score first, and
show each rider's own count of cells laid and traps sprung. Your records keep your wins, losses,
best winning margin and longest ride for each channel, opponent and Kid setting.

## Patches

- **Club**:
  - First Wall: win a match against anyone.
  - Long Ride: ride 60 seconds in one point on a speed-up channel.
- **Pro**:
  - Boxed In: win a point by shutting a rival in ten cells or fewer. Opens Fading Trails.
  - Clean Sweep: win a match against a Kid without losing a point. Opens Shrinking Arena.
  - Old Machine: beat Computer 1977 with its switch on A. Opens Boost Lanes.
- **Legend**: Faster Than Lou: beat Grandpa Lou in an eight-way speed-up channel with your switch
  on A. Opens Four Way and Ghost Trails.
- **Secret**:
  - "The old riders never quite touched."
  - "Some trails are worth keeping."

## Den Life

- **Difficulty switches**: each seat's switch decides what turning straight back does. On B it is
  ignored; on A it goes through, into your own trail. On the computer's channels, the left switch
  sets how far Computer 1977 looks ahead: 3 cells on B, 6 on A. A flip applies from the next match.
- **Gold thread**: any patch earned with your switch on A is sewn in gold. Faster Than Lou always
  is, since it asks for A.
- **The Neighbourhood Kids**: every Kid reacts with human reflexes: they react late, see only so
  far, miss turns and fumble.
  - **Grandpa Lou** reads the whole board but turns a beat late, and he falls behind when the pace
    climbs.
  - **Moss** claims the big side early and keeps clear of trouble.
  - **Rosa** hunts you and cuts across your path.
  - **Benny** copies your turns a moment after you make them.
  - **Dee** has the quickest hands and gets stronger as the pace climbs, but she boxes herself in.
  - **Pip** wanders, then springs a trap out of nowhere.

  The Den's Kid setting, _Human_ or _Sharp_, also covers reflexes: Sharp Kids never fumble and
  react twice as fast.

- **Power Cycle Run**, 45 seconds each:
  - Hold on at top speed: ride 30 seconds at top speed.
  - Box Dee in: shut Dee in a pocket once.
  - Fill 300 cells: lay 300 cells riding alone.
  - Win at the edges: win a point in wraparound.
- **Versus Night**: Trailblaze takes part as a two-player duel. A Kid can take seat 2.
- **Daily share line**: one square per point, 🟦 yours, 🟥 your rival's, ⬜ both, then the score:
  `Trailblaze · 🟦🟦🟥⬜🟦 · 10–6`.

## Tips

- Turn early at speed: the faster it gets, the more cells pass before your hands react.
- Cut across your rival a few cells ahead of their bike, never right in front of it.
- Claim the big side first, then fill your own space tightly along the walls.
- On wraparound channels the edges are open: a rival can come at you from behind.
- Against Computer 1977, lead it into a pocket. It only ever looks a few cells ahead.
