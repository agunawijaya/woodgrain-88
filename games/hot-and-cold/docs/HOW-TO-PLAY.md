# Hot & Cold — How to play

_The player's manual: goal, controls, clues, channels, settings, scoring, patches and Den Life._

## Goal

Find the hidden flag. Walk your explorer to a square and check it; the clue it shows tells you how
far away the flag is or which way it lies. Check the flag's own square to capture it.

## Controls

| Action                        | Alone, or seat 1                    | Seat 2 (shared keyboard)     | Gamepad                  |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Walk                          | Arrow keys, or keypad 8 4 6 2       | W A S D                      | Stick or d-pad           |
| Walk diagonally               | Keypad 7 9 1 3, or two keys at once | Q E Z C, or two keys at once | Stick or d-pad diagonals |
| Check the square you stand on | Enter                               | Space                        | A                        |
| Walk there, then check        | Click a square, click it again      | —                            | —                        |
| Pause                         | Esc                                 | Esc                          | Start                    |

The field wraps on every edge: walk off one side and you come back in at the other. Two explorers
can never stand on the same square. With the mouse, your explorer walks the shortest way, round the
edges if that is shorter, on the same clock as the keys. The mouse is off in a two-person race,
where one mouse cannot be fair to both.

## The clues

| Clue             | What it says                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A number, 1 to 8 | How many steps away the flag is, counting diagonal steps as one. A 1 means the flag is right beside it. The number never counts round the edges.           |
| An arrow         | Which way the flag lies. It points along the row or column when the flag shares it, and otherwise diagonally, however steep the true line.                 |
| A bomb           | A short blast, and your explorer goes back to its home corner (top left for seat 1, bottom right for seat 2), unless the other explorer is standing there. |
| The flag         | Captured. A flag on a bomb square is captured safely: the flag is checked first.                                                                           |

Two clues together narrow it down quickly: where a number's ring crosses an arrow's wedge, the flag
must be.

**Clue modes.**

- **Notebook** (the default) keeps every clue on the field.
- **Classic Memory** shows a clue only while you hold the check button, as in 1978, so you have to
  remember the rest.

## Modes and channels

From the game menu, pick a channel, then a mode: **vs a Kid**, **Versus** (two people, one
keyboard) or **Alone**. The ten cartridge variations are channels, numbered as on the cartridge:

| #   | Channel          | Group | How it plays                               | The flag                                       | The end                                     |
| --- | ---------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Race             | Race  | Both seats at once                         | Still                                          | First to 15 flags                           |
| 2   | Turns            | Turns | One check each, in turn                    | Still                                          | First to 15                                 |
| 3   | Turns · Bouncing | Turns | One check each                             | Moves after every miss, bouncing off the edges | First to 15                                 |
| 4   | Turns · Wrapping | Turns | One check each                             | Moves after every miss, wrapping round         | First to 75                                 |
| 5   | Hunt for Hunt    | Turns | Whole hunts in turn; every check counts    | Still                                          | A seat's checks reach 75; fewer checks wins |
| 6   | Hunt · Bouncing  | Turns | Whole hunts in turn                        | Bouncing                                       | As 5                                        |
| 7   | Hunt · Wrapping  | Turns | Whole hunts in turn                        | Wrapping                                       | As 5                                        |
| 8   | Clock            | Solo  | Alone against a 75-tick clock (about 92 s) | Still                                          | Time runs out; count your flags             |
| 9   | Clock · Bouncing | Solo  | Alone                                      | Bouncing                                       | Time runs out                               |
| 10  | Clock · Wrapping | Solo  | Alone                                      | Wrapping                                       | Time runs out                               |

In turns, after a capture the seat that found the flag sits out the start of the next field. In hunt
for hunt, when the first seat reaches 75 checks, the second seat gets one last hunt, so both have
had the same number of turns.

**Remix channels** open as you earn patches:

- **Two Flags**: two flags hide at once, and every clue points to the nearer one.
- **Minefield**: twice the bombs, fewer arrows.
- **Thin Ice**: clues stay on the field for ten seconds, then melt away.
- **Drift Storm**: the flag drifts two squares at every miss, bouncing off the edges.

## Settings

- **Look**: Polar Expedition, Tabletop Board or Radar Room. The rules are the same in all three,
  and you can switch from the pause menu mid-match.
- **Clues**: Notebook or Classic Memory. A change from the pause menu applies from the next match.
- **Possibility fog**: shades the squares that can no longer hold the flag. It is off and locked in
  the daily, challenge links, the Power Cycle Run and Versus Night. A run with it on counts as
  played but never becomes a record.
- **Match length**: the cartridge's (15, or 75 for channel 4) or Short (first to 5).
- **Clue colours** and **seat colours**: standard, or colour-blind-safe.
- **Effects**: full, or quietened. **Effects volume.**

## Scoring

- **Race, turns and the clock**: one point per flag; the most flags wins.
- **Hunt for hunt**: every check counts against the seat that makes it; the fewer checks wins.
- **The daily**: one hunt, and fewer checks is better. It scores 25 less the checks taken (never
  below 1), and its share line says the checks plainly.

## Patches

- **Club**:
  - Flag Finder: find 10 flags, in any channel.
  - Neighbour's Rival: win a race or a turns match against any Neighbourhood Kid.
- **Pro**:
  - Bomb Dodger: find 5 flags in a row without setting off a bomb. Opens Minefield.
  - Sharp Eyes: hunting alone, find 3 flags with your first 12 checks. Opens Thin Ice.
  - Pure Memory: win a turns match against Moss in Classic Memory. Opens Two Flags.
- **Legend**: Old Fox: beat Grandpa Lou with a moving flag, in Classic Memory, with your switch on
  A. Opens Drift Storm.
- **Secret**: "Some finds come right at the last moment."

## Den Life

- **Difficulty switches**: switch A slows your steps by a quarter in the race (channel 1) and counts
  every check twice in hunt for hunt (5 to 7). In the turns and clock channels it does nothing, as on
  the cartridge.
- **Gold thread**: a patch earned with your switch on A is sewn in gold. Old Fox always is.
- **The Neighbourhood Kids** hunt with human memory. They forget what they found, mix clues up and
  miss what you uncovered. After a match, "What Lou remembered" shows it.
  - **Grandpa Lou** remembers nearly everything.
  - **Moss** is careful.
  - **Rosa** and **Benny** watch your finds closely.
  - **Dee** is quick and forgetful.
  - **Pip** forgets half and mixes up the rest.

  The Den's Kid setting, _Human_ or _Sharp_, decides how they play: Sharp Kids remember
  everything, never take a wrong step and think twice as fast. Sharp results are marked "Sharp
  Kids" on records and share lines.

- **Power Cycle Run**, 45 seconds each:
  - Find the flag.
  - Find 2 flags.
  - A flag without a bomb: find a flag without setting off a bomb on its field.
  - Beat Dee to the flag.
- **Versus Night**: Hot & Cold takes part, with two people on one keyboard or a Kid in seat 2.
- **Daily share line**: one symbol per check (🔢 distance, ➡️ arrow, 💥 bomb, 🚩 flag, at most 12),
  then the checks: `Hot & Cold · 🔢➡️💥🔢🚩 · 5 checks`.

## Tips

- A number of 1 or 2 is gold: the flag is very close. Check round it before walking away.
- Cross a number with an arrow; one of each usually leaves only a few squares.
- On moving channels the flag drifts after every miss, so trust your newest clues most.
- Bombs cost you the walk home, not the clues you found.
- In turns, the race is not to the flag but to the last missing clue.
